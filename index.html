        How much protein do you need?

        Most adults need at least 0.8 g of protein for every kilogram they weigh (about 0.36 g per pound). That’s the minimum to stay healthy, not the best amount for everyone.

        If you exercise regularly, 1.2 to 1.6 g per kg helps your muscles recover. If you’re building muscle or losing weight and want to keep your muscle, research supports 1.6 to 2.2 g per kg.

        It’s easier to hit your goal by spreading protein across the day. Aim for about 20 to 40 g at each meal instead of one big serving at dinner.

        Common questions

        How much protein do I need a day?

        At least 0.8 g per kg of body weight for most adults. Active people often do better with 1.2 to 1.6 g per kg, and people building muscle or losing weight with 1.6 to 2.2 g per kg. Use the calculator above for your number.

        Is it better to spread protein out?

        Yes. Three to four meals with 20 to 40 g each is a practical way to reach your goal and helps your muscles repair.

        Can I eat too much protein?

        For healthy adults, up to about 2 g per kg a day is generally considered safe. If you have kidney disease, follow your doctor’s advice on protein.
